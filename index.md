Privacy Policy for PhotoLite

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

App Name: PhotoLite
Developer: RahiVerse Studio
Package Name: com.rahiverse.photolite

RahiVerse Studio ("we", "us", or "our") developed PhotoLite ("the App") as a photo compression and resizing application.

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoLite accesses, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the App.

By using PhotoLite, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Access

PhotoLite may request access to photos and images stored on your device.

This access is required so that you can:

Select photos for compression.
Resize photos.
Process photos using the App's features.
Save processed photos to your device.

PhotoLite only accesses photos necessary for the features you choose to use.

Photos and Images

Photos selected by you are processed on your device for the purpose of providing the App's functionality.

PhotoLite does not upload your photos or images to our servers.

We do not sell, rent, or share your photos with third parties.

2. Information We Collect

PhotoLite is designed to operate without collecting personally identifiable information from you.

We do not intentionally collect:

Your name
Postal address
Phone number
Email address
Contacts
Location information
Photos or images for storage on our servers
Photo contents or image metadata for our own purposes
Account information
Payment information

PhotoLite does not require you to create an account to use its core functionality.

3. How Your Photos Are Used

When you select a photo in PhotoLite, the photo may be temporarily processed in the device's memory or local storage to perform operations such as:

Compression
Resizing
Image processing
Saving the resulting image

The processing is performed locally on your device.

PhotoLite does not transmit your selected photos to RahiVerse Studio or a third-party server.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or photos with third parties.

PhotoLite does not provide your photos to advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

If this practice changes in a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Internet and Network Access

The current version of PhotoLite is designed to perform its photo-processing functionality locally on your device.

The current version does not require an internet connection for its core photo compression and resizing functionality.

6. Permissions

PhotoLite may request Android permissions related to accessing photos or images.

These permissions are used solely to provide the App's photo-selection and photo-processing functionality.

You can manage or revoke permissions through your Android device's settings. Some features may not function correctly if the required permission is denied.

7. Data Storage and Retention

PhotoLite does not maintain a remote database containing your photos or personal information.

Photos created or saved by PhotoLite remain under your control on your device.

Files saved by the App may remain on your device until you choose to delete them using your device's file manager, gallery application, or the App's available deletion functionality, if applicable.

Uninstalling PhotoLite may remove application-specific data according to the Android operating system's normal app-data management behavior.

8. Data Security

Because PhotoLite processes photos locally on your device and does not upload them to our servers, your photos are not transmitted to RahiVerse Studio for storage or processing.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock and keeping Android and the App updated.

9. Children's Privacy

PhotoLite is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the contact information below.

10. Third-Party Services

The current version of PhotoLite does not intentionally share user photos or personal information with third-party services.

If third-party services, SDKs, advertising services, analytics services, or other technologies are added in future versions and they collect or process user data, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated before or when such functionality is introduced, as applicable.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You can control PhotoLite's access to photos through Android's permission settings.

You may:

Deny photo access.
Revoke previously granted permissions.
Delete photos created or saved by the App.
Uninstall PhotoLite from your device.
