PhotoLite

Simple, fast and private photo compression & resizing.

Privacy Policy · Last Updated September 23, 2026

Privacy at a Glance

PhotoLite is designed with privacy in mind. The app allows you to compress and resize photos directly on your device.

Your photos stay on your device.
PhotoLite does not upload your selected photos to RahiVerse Studio or store your photos on our servers.

1. About This Privacy Policy

RahiVerse Studio ("we", "us", or "our") developed PhotoLite ("the App"), a photo compression and resizing application.

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoLite accesses, uses, stores and handles information when you use the App.

Developer: RahiVerse Studio
App: PhotoLite
Package: com.rahiverse.photolite

2. Information We Access

PhotoLite may request access to photos and images stored on your device. This access is required so you can select photos and use the App's compression and resizing features.

PhotoLite may use photo access to:

  • Select photos for compression.
  • Resize photos.
  • Process photos locally.
  • Save processed photos to your device.
PhotoLite does not upload your photos to our servers.

3. Photos and Images

Photos selected by you are processed on your device for the purpose of providing PhotoLite's functionality.

PhotoLite does not sell, rent or share your photos with third parties.

We do not use your photos for advertising, profiling or unrelated purposes.

4. Information We Collect

PhotoLite is designed to operate without collecting personally identifiable information from you.

We do not intentionally collect:

  • Your name
  • Postal address
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Contacts
  • Location information
  • Your photos on our servers
  • Photo contents for our own purposes
  • Account information
  • Payment information

PhotoLite does not require you to create an account to use its core functionality.

5. How Your Photos Are Used

When you select a photo in PhotoLite, the photo may be temporarily processed in your device's memory or local storage to perform operations such as:

  • Compression
  • Resizing
  • Image processing
  • Saving the resulting image

These operations are performed locally on your device.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information or photos with third parties.

PhotoLite does not provide your photos to advertisers, analytics providers or other third parties.

7. Internet and Network Access

The current version of PhotoLite is designed to perform its core photo compression and resizing functionality locally on your device.

The core photo-processing functionality does not require an internet connection.

8. App Permissions

PhotoLite may request Android permissions related to accessing photos or images.

These permissions are used to provide the App's photo-selection and photo-processing functionality.

You can manage or revoke permissions through your Android device's settings. Some features may not function correctly if the required permission is denied.

9. Data Storage and Retention

PhotoLite does not maintain a remote database containing your photos or personal information.

Photos created or saved by PhotoLite remain under your control on your device.

Files saved by the App may remain on your device until you choose to delete them using your device's file manager, gallery application or applicable App functionality.

10. Data Security

Because PhotoLite processes photos locally on your device and does not upload them to our servers, your photos are not transmitted to RahiVerse Studio for storage or processing.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock and keeping Android and PhotoLite updated.

11. Children's Privacy

PhotoLite is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the information below.

12. Third-Party Services

The current version of PhotoLite does not intentionally share user photos or personal information with third-party services.

If third-party services, advertising services, analytics services or other technologies are added in future versions and they collect or process user data, this Privacy Policy and applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

13. Your Privacy Choices

You can control PhotoLite's access to photos through Android's permission settings.

  • Deny photo access.
  • Revoke previously granted permissions.
  • Delete photos created or saved by the App.
  • Uninstall PhotoLite from your device.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to PhotoLite, applicable laws or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the Effective Date at the top of this page will be updated.
